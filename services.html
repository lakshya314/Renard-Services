01Digital Signature

Digital Signature Certificate

A Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) is the electronic equivalent of your physical signature, issued by a Certifying Authority licensed in India. It lets you sign documents and file returns online securely and legally. We've been issuing DSCs since 2013, and we can deliver yours in just 24 hours.

DSC Types

Class 3 – IndividualFor professionals, directors and individuals filing on government portals.
Class 3 – OrganisationIssued in the name of an authorised signatory of a company or firm.
DGFT DSCFor importers and exporters using the DGFT portal with an IEC.
Signing + EncryptionCombo DSC required for e-tendering and secure document exchange.

Where You Can Use It

MCA / ROC filingsGST returnsIncome Tax (ITR)e-TenderingEPFODGFTICEGATEe-ProcurementSigning PDFs

02Trademark

Trademark Registration

A trademark can be a word, phrase, symbol, design, or a combination of these that identifies your brand. Registering it gives you legal ownership and stops others from using a similar mark. We handle the entire process, from search to registration.

What We Offer

Trademark SearchCheck availability and conflicts before you file.
Application FilingFiling in the right class, out of 45 classes, with the Trademark Registry.
Objection ReplyDrafting replies to examination reports.
Opposition SupportHelp when your application is opposed by a third party.
Trademark RenewalRenew every 10 years to keep your protection active.
Assignment & TransferTransfer ownership of a registered trademark.
